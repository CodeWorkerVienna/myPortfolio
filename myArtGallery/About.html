About the Gallery

Our gallery is a place where art meets curiosity. We show works from many different art forms and believe that everyone can find something that speaks to them, whether they visit for the first time or come back every week.

Our collection ranges from modern art, sculpture and photography to abstract works, landscapes and portraits. Installations, digital art and street art complete the picture and show how varied art can be today.

Every exhibition is built around one theme and presents a carefully chosen selection of works. Our current featured exhibition, Many Voices, brings together nine art forms and invites you to see how differently artists look at the world.

About the artist

Damjan is the curator behind this gallery. He is fascinated by the way a single image or object can tell a story without words, and he enjoys bringing very different styles together in one place.

His goal is to make art accessible: clear presentation, honest descriptions and a space where visitors can take their time and form their own opinion.
